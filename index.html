ENGINEERING NOTES

ZVD timing: [0, T/2, T] with weights [¼, ½, ¼]. Identical trolley and pendulum models; gravity 9.81 m/s². The ZVD controller preserves commanded travel distance but extends the move by one natural swing period. Small-angle, fixed-length input shaping is nominal; the simulator integrates a nonlinear pendulum with light damping.

*Current is a simplified traction-torque estimate from acceleration, payload reaction, friction, drivetrain gearing, efficiency, and motor torque constant. No inverter electrical dynamics, current limits, hoist motion, load elasticity, wind, or feedback sensor noise are modeled. Not for commissioning or safety calculations.
